What's inside

Made for the age where a child wants to touch everything on the page.

  • Every page read aloud, in the same voice all the way through
  • Tap any word to hear it on its own — for children just starting to read
  • Your child's name on the title page
  • Eighteen games woven into the story, not bolted on the end
  • Story only, or story with games — switch whenever you like
  • Works offline, on a plane or in the car
  • No ads, no data collected, nothing to buy once you own it
Everything stays on your device. The app has no internet connection at all — no accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Read the privacy policy.
